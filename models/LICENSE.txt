symbol-classifier.onnx
======================

A format conversion of `models/cnn_aug.keras` from
https://github.com/altynbk/handwritten-math-recognition
(commit 3d91c0c503a596b4efbf35da36cf00e931cc0928).

The weights are unchanged. They were copied from the Keras archive into an ONNX graph
by scripts/model/convert_model.py; no training or fine-tuning was performed.

digit-helper-mathex.onnx
========================

A format conversion of `models/mathex_v1.h5` from
https://github.com/devansh9837/mathex
(commit 7341d9e95309f4d427514dde878d5557a4206e4a).

The weights are unchanged. They were copied from the Keras file into an ONNX graph by
scripts/model/convert_helpers.py; no training or fine-tuning was performed. On 1,000
test images the ONNX copy and the Keras original agree to within 0.000005 on every
output.

digit-helper-mnist.onnx
=======================

`mnist-12.onnx` from the ONNX Model Zoo, unchanged, byte for byte
(sha256 5c688690f8bacf667d4c2074af5ad0646ca328d7ab03eccf944a65b320171bdd).

  https://github.com/onnx/models/tree/main/validated/vision/classification/mnist
  https://huggingface.co/onnxmodelzoo/mnist-12

The model was trained on the MNIST data set with CNTK, following the tutorial
"CNTK 103D: Convolutional Neural Network with MNIST".

Its README in the Model Zoo states "License: MIT". No separate licence text or copyright
line accompanies the model. The repository that publishes it, github.com/onnx/models, is
under the Apache License 2.0, and the Hugging Face mirror labels the model apache-2.0.
Both licences permit redistribution with attribution, which this notice gives.
