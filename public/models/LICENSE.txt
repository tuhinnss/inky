symbol-classifier.onnx
======================

A format conversion of `models/cnn_aug.keras` from
https://github.com/altynbk/handwritten-math-recognition
(commit 3d91c0c503a596b4efbf35da36cf00e931cc0928).

The weights are unchanged. They were copied from the Keras archive into an ONNX graph
by scripts/model/convert_model.py; no training or fine-tuning was performed.
